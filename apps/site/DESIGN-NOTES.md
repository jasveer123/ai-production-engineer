# Pramaan: evidence in focus

The homepage introduces Pramaan as an AI SRE through a layered incident workspace, a floating AI finding and inspectable logs, traces and a candidate patch. The composition tilts into a flat readable view as it enters the viewport. It respects reduced motion and uses a stacked composition on mobile.

## Visual direction and provenance

Hobbes informed the use of contextual product scenes, layered panels and soft shadows; its artwork, copy and implementation were not reused. The 21st MCP located Container Scroll Animation (component 1081, manuarora700). Source retrieval was blocked by the daily retrieval limit, so the perspective implementation was independently authored with CSS transforms and a passive, requestAnimationFrame-backed scroll listener.

The prompt was written in ART-DIRECTION.md before generation. The original transparent optical sculpture, public/visuals/evidence-optics.png, illustrates separate context layers bringing evidence into focus. It replaces the wave landscape in the current homepage. Precise interfaces and data illustrations remain authored HTML, SVG and CSS.

## Page changes

- Hero: layered incident window, floating AI finding, interactive evidence tabs, scroll perspective and soft shadows.
- Second section: original optical sculpture paired with context, evidence and review steps.
- Supporting scenes: restrained perspective, shadows and lower-edge fades.
- Integrations: three working source categories, setup links and context contributions, reused on the dedicated integrations page.
- Both colour palettes adapt the entire composition; mobile layouts avoid horizontal overflow.

The examples are illustrative. They do not run a live AI request, validate a patch, connect a source or change production. Actual functionality is reached through the separate workspace, subject to its deployment configuration.
